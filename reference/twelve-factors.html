Twelve-factor agents

Dex Horthy / HumanLayer. Opinionated production checklist, not a spec. Source.

Compression Own the tokens (1–4), the lifetime (5–8), and the failure (9–12).
#FactorOne line
1Natural language → tool callsA sentence becomes structured intent; your code takes it from there.
2Own your promptsThe string is source code. If a framework hides it, you cannot debug.
3Own your context window“Here’s what happened; what’s the next step?” Pack it yourself.
4Tools are structured outputsJSON in. Your switch decides whether — and how — to honor it.
5Unify execution and business stateOne event log. Do not let the transcript and the ticket drift.
6Launch / pause / resumeSerialize the thread. Wake on a webhook. Do not sleep in workers.
7Contact humans with tool callsHITL is an intent that pauses the loop.
8Own your control flowWhich intents continue, which break, which wait before invoke.
9Compact errors into contextShort observation, retry cap, then escalate.
10Small, focused agentsRoughly 3–20 steps. Scope is a context strategy.
11Trigger from anywhereSlack, email, cron, webhook. A service, not a chat box.
12Stateless reducernext = reduce(events). Durable state lives outside the process.
