Lesson 0019 · Phase 5: Career · ~15 minutes

Read a real ship

Decode someone else’s agent with the vocabulary you now have. This is how you talk in interviews about systems you did not write.

Your one win today Apply a seven-question reading protocol to Anthropic’s Research system (you already have the post from 0015) so that “I read a production write-up” becomes a specific, cited story, not a vibe.

1 · Why read ships

Portfolio work you build yourself will be small at first. Interviewers also want to hear you navigate a system at production scale. The honest way, without pretending you work at Anthropic: read the engineering post like a code review, with this course’s terms as the lint rules.

Wisdom, later, comes from communities — r/LocalLLaMA, AI Engineer talks — where people argue about ships in public. This lesson is the reading skill those rooms assume.[1]

2 · The seven questions

  1. Agent or workflow? Who picks the next step? Research: lead is an agent; workers are agents; the topology (spawn parallel, synthesize) is a workflow the lead sits in. Mixed. Say so.
  2. What is the harness? What runs tools, persists, stops? If the post is silent, that silence is data — you would ask in a design review.
  3. What is the ACI? Which tools, how described, what is forbidden? Research: search tools; subagent-spawn is itself a tool. Compression is the job of the worker’s final message.
  4. Where is memory? Isolated windows per subagent (0015). Lead never sees raw dumps — it sees compressed returns. That is context isolation, not a vector DB.
  5. How do they know it works? Internal research eval, +90.2% vs single-agent, BrowseComp variance (tokens 80%). They eval. Quote the numbers.
  6. What does it cost? 4× / 15×. They published it. Most posts will not. Demand it anyway.
  7. When would you not copy this? Colliding writes; enumerable paths; no baseline. You already have this answer (0015). Saying it is the senior move.

4 · Retrieval practice

1. Anthropic Research is “mixed” because…

Correct. Topology is yours (orchestrator–workers). Nodes are tools-in-a-loop. Mixed is the precise word.
Not languages, not fine-tunes. Agent nodes in a workflow shape. Option 3.

2. The memory trick in that system is…

Correct. Compression is the point of subagents. A global store or an ever-growing lead window is the thing they avoided.
They isolated on purpose. Don't undo it with a shared bag of chunks. Option 1.

3. A senior closer after praising the architecture?

Correct. Admiration plus a no. That is design judgment, which is the skill.
90.2% is quality, not price (15×). Refunds are colliding, irreversible state. Option 2.

4. You have not worked at Anthropic. How do you still use this post?

Correct. Honest scope: I read the engineering post; here is what I took; here is what I would not copy. That is professional.
Do not lie, and the post is public. Cite and caveat. Option 3.
